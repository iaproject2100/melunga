# Niveau 7 « Expliquer et comparer » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Expliquer une raison, comparer et exprimer une préférence.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6.

## Espagnol (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Approximate English respelling for English-speaking readers (CAPITALS = stressed syllable; vowels ah / eh / ee / oh / oo; j and g before e/i → kh as in Scottish 'loch'; ñ → ny; ll → y; c before e/i and z → s; r is rolled; monosyllables in capitals). Standard Castilian with seseo and yeísmo. No substitute for the audio.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended.

### L7-en-es-d1 · Choosing a flat

- **A** : ¿Qué piso prefieres, el del centro o el de las afueras? — KEH PEE-soh preh-FEEEH-rehs, EHL DEHL SEHN-troh OH EHL DEH LAHS ah-FOOEH-rahs — Which flat do you prefer, the one in the centre or the one in the suburbs?
- **B** : Prefiero el del centro porque está cerca del trabajo. — preh-FEEEH-roh EHL DEHL SEHN-troh POHR-keh ehs-TAH SEHR-kah DEHL trah-BAH-khoh — I prefer the one in the centre because it's close to work.
- **A** : Pero es más caro que el otro. — PEH-roh EHS MAHS KAH-roh KEH EHL OH-troh — But it's more expensive than the other one.
- **B** : Es verdad, pero es menos ruidoso y tiene más luz. — EHS vehr-DAHD, PEH-roh EHS MEH-nohs rooee-DOH-soh EE TEEEH-neh MAHS LOOS — That's true, but it's less noisy and it gets more light.
- **A** : ¿Y el barrio? — EE EHL BAH-rreeoh — And the neighbourhood?
- **B** : Lo bueno es que hay un mercado y un parque al lado. — LOH BOOEH-noh EHS KEH AHEE OON mehr-KAH-doh EE OON PAHR-keh AHL LAH-doh — The good thing is that there's a market and a park next door.
- **A** : Creo que tienes razón. — KREH-oh KEH TEEEH-nehs rah-SOHN — I think you're right.
- **B** : Antes vivía en las afueras y perdía mucho tiempo en el coche. — AHN-tehs vee-VEE-ah EHN LAHS ah-FOOEH-rahs EE pehr-DEE-ah MOO-choh TEEEHM-poh EHN EHL KOH-cheh — I used to live in the suburbs and I wasted a lot of time in the car.

### L7-en-es-d2 · At the restaurant

- **A** : ¿Qué te parece este restaurante? — KEH TEH pah-REH-seh EHS-teh rehs-tahoo-RAHN-teh — What do you think of this restaurant?
- **B** : Me parece muy bueno, pero un poco caro. — MEH pah-REH-seh MOOEE BOOEH-noh, PEH-roh OON POH-koh KAH-roh — I think it's very good, but a bit expensive.
- **A** : ¿Qué vas a pedir? — KEH VAHS AH peh-DEER — What are you going to order?
- **B** : El pescado. Me gusta más el pescado que la carne. — EHL pehs-KAH-doh. MEH GOOS-tah MAHS EHL pehs-KAH-doh KEH LAH KAHR-neh — The fish. I like fish better than meat.
- **A** : Yo prefiero la paella porque es más típica. — YOH preh-FEEEH-roh LAH pah-EH-yah POHR-keh EHS MAHS TEE-pee-kah — I prefer the paella because it's more typical.
- **B** : Buena idea. ¿Y de beber? — BOOEH-nah ee-DEH-ah. EE DEH beh-BEHR — Good idea. And to drink?
- **A** : Para mí, agua. Antes bebía vino en la comida, ahora ya no. — PAH-rah MEE, AH-gooah. AHN-tehs beh-BEE-ah VEE-noh EHN LAH koh-MEE-dah, ah-OH-rah YAH NOH — For me, water. I used to drink wine at lunch, but not any more.
- **B** : Yo también bebo menos que antes. — YOH tahm-BEEEHN BEH-boh MEH-nohs KEH AHN-tehs — I drink less than before too.

### L7-en-es-d3 · Working from home or at the office

- **A** : ¿Trabajas desde casa? — trah-BAH-khahs DEHS-deh KAH-sah — Do you work from home?
- **B** : Sí, tres días por semana. Para mí, es más práctico. — SEE, TREHS DEE-ahs POHR seh-MAH-nah. PAH-rah MEE, EHS MAHS PRAHK-tee-koh — Yes, three days a week. For me, it's more practical.
- **A** : ¿Por qué? — POHR KEH — Why?
- **B** : Es que pierdo menos tiempo en el transporte y estoy más tranquilo. — EHS KEH PEEEHR-doh MEH-nohs TEEEHM-poh EHN EHL trahns-POHR-teh EE ehs-TOHEE MAHS trahn-KEE-loh — It's just that I waste less time commuting and I'm more relaxed.
- **A** : Yo prefiero ir a la oficina porque veo a mis compañeros. — YOH preh-FEEEH-roh EER AH LAH oh-fee-SEE-nah POHR-keh VEH-oh AH MEES kohm-pah-NYEH-rohs — I prefer going to the office because I see my colleagues.
- **B** : Te entiendo. Lo bueno es que ahora podemos elegir. — TEH ehn-TEEEHN-doh. LOH BOOEH-noh EHS KEH ah-OH-rah poh-DEH-mohs eh-leh-KHEER — I understand. The good thing is that now we can choose.
- **A** : Antes trabajaba todos los días en la oficina y llegaba a casa muy tarde. — AHN-tehs trah-bah-KHAH-bah TOH-dohs LOHS DEE-ahs EHN LAH oh-fee-SEE-nah EE yeh-GAH-bah AH KAH-sah MOOEE TAHR-deh — I used to work at the office every day and I got home very late.
- **B** : Ahora descansas más que antes, ¿no? — ah-OH-rah dehs-KAHN-sahs MAHS KEH AHN-tehs, NOH — Now you rest more than before, don't you?
- **A** : Sí, mucho más. — SEE, MOO-choh MAHS — Yes, much more.

*Acquis attendu* : Justify a choice (porque, es que), compare (más / menos… que, mejor), give a simple opinion (creo que, me parece, para mí) and describe a past habit in the imperfect to explain a change.

### L7-en-zh-d2 · Tastes at the table

- **A** : 你更喜欢中国菜还是法国菜？ *(nǐ gèng xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài háishi Fǎguó cài?)* — nee gung shee-hwahn joong-gwoh tseye heye-shr fah-gwoh tseye? — Do you prefer Chinese food or French food?

- **B** : 对，而且离公司更近。 *(duì, érqiě lí gōngsī gèng jìn.)* — dway, ar-chyeh lee goong-suh gung jeen. — Yes, and it's closer to the office.
- **A** : 我觉得这里的服务也很好。 *(wǒ juéde zhèlǐ de fúwù yě hěn hǎo.)* — woh jyweh-duh juh-lee duh foo-oo yeh hun how. — I think the service here is very good too.

### L7-en-zh-d3 · Why move?

- **A** : 你为什么搬家？ *(nǐ wèishénme bānjiā?)* — nee way-shun-muh bahn-jyah? — Why are you moving?
- **B** : 因为以前的公寓太小了，所以我们找了一个大一点的。 *(yīnwèi yǐqián de gōngyù tài xiǎo le, suǒyǐ wǒmen zhǎo le yí ge dà yìdiǎn de.)* — een-way ee-chyen duh goong-yu teye shyow luh, swoh-ee woh-mun jow luh ee guh dah ee-dyen duh. — Because the old apartment was too small, so we found a slightly bigger one.
- **A** : 新家在哪里？ *(xīn jiā zài nǎlǐ?)* — sheen jyah dzeye nah-lee? — Where's the new place?
- **B** : 在郊区。比市中心安静，也更便宜。 *(zài jiāoqū. bǐ shì zhōngxīn ānjìng, yě gèng piányi.)* — dzeye jyow-chyu. bee shr joong-sheen ahn-jing, yeh gung pyen-ee. — In the suburbs. It's quieter than downtown, and cheaper too.
- **A** : 但是上班不方便吧？ *(dànshì shàngbān bù fāngbiàn ba?)* — dahn-shr shahng-bahn boo fahng-byen bah? — But getting to work isn't convenient, is it?
- **B** : 以前我走路上班，现在坐地铁，四十分钟。 *(yǐqián wǒ zǒulù shàngbān, xiànzài zuò dìtiě, sìshí fēnzhōng.)* — ee-chyen woh dzoh-loo shahng-bahn, shyen-dzeye dzwoh dee-tyeh, suh-shr fun-joong. — I used to walk to work, now I take the subway, forty minutes.
- **A** : 我觉得有点远。 *(wǒ juéde yǒudiǎn yuǎn.)* — woh jyweh-duh yoh-dyen ywen. — I think that's a bit far.
- **B** : 是有点远，不过我们有一个大阳台！ *(shì yǒudiǎn yuǎn, búguò wǒmen yǒu yí ge dà yángtái!)* — shr yoh-dyen ywen, boo-gwoh woh-mun yoh ee guh dah yahng-teye! — It is a bit far, but we have a big balcony!

*Acquis attendu* : You can give reasons with 因为… 所以…, compare with 比 (and 没有 for 'not as… as'), argue a preference (更喜欢…，因为…), give an opinion (我觉得) and contrast before and now (以前… 现在…了), recognizing the change-of-state 了.

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o). The u of de-su / ma-su is almost silent ('dess', 'mass'); r is a light tap between r and l; g is always hard. The reading field gives the hiragana. Polite register (です / ます) throughout.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended; linguistic review required (naturalness of phrasing, kanji readings).

- **B** : 前は肉をたくさん食べていましたが、今は魚をよく食べます。 *(まえはにくをたくさんたべていましたが、いまはさかなをよくたべます。)* — ma-e wa ni-ku o ta-ku-sa-n ta-be-te i-ma-shi-ta ga, i-ma wa sa-ka-na o yo-ku ta-be-ma-su — I used to eat a lot of meat, but now I often eat fish.

### L7-en-ja-d3 · At work: explaining a choice

- **A** : 田中さん、今日は早く帰りますか。 *(たなかさん、きょうははやくかえりますか。)* — ta-na-ka-sa-n, kyo-o wa ha-ya-ku ka-e-ri-ma-su ka — Mr Tanaka, are you leaving early today?
- **B** : はい、子供を迎えに行くので、五時に帰ります。 *(はい、こどもをむかえにいくので、ごじにかえります。)* — ha-i, ko-do-mo o mu-ka-e ni i-ku no-de, go-ji ni ka-e-ri-ma-su — Yes, I'm picking up my child, so I'm leaving at five.
- **A** : そうですか。田中さんは車で来ますか。 *(そうですか。たなかさんはくるまできますか。)* — so-o de-su ka. ta-na-ka-sa-n wa ku-ru-ma de ki-ma-su ka — I see. Do you come by car?
- **B** : いいえ、電車で来ます。電車の方が速いと思います。 *(いいえ、でんしゃできます。でんしゃのほうがはやいとおもいます。)* — i-i-e, de-n-sha de ki-ma-su. de-n-sha no ho-o ga ha-ya-i to o-mo-i-ma-su — No, I come by train. I think the train is faster.
- **A** : 私は自転車です。安いし、健康にいいからです。 *(わたしはじてんしゃです。やすいし、けんこうにいいからです。)* — wa-ta-shi wa ji-te-n-sha de-su. ya-su-i shi, ke-n-ko-o ni i-i ka-ra de-su — I come by bike. Because it's cheap and good for your health.
- **B** : いいですね。前は私も自転車に乗っていましたが、今は遠いので電車です。 *(いいですね。まえはわたしもじてんしゃにのっていましたが、いまはとおいのででんしゃです。)* — i-i de-su ne. ma-e wa wa-ta-shi mo ji-te-n-sha ni not-te i-ma-shi-ta ga, i-ma wa to-o-i no-de de-n-sha de-su — That's nice. I used to ride a bike too, but now it's far, so I take the train.
- **A** : 引っ越しましたか。 *(ひっこしましたか。)* — hik-ko-shi-ma-shi-ta ka — Did you move?
