# Niveau 8 « Demander et négocier » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Demander poliment, exprimer une condition et négocier simplement.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L6, L7.

## Espagnol (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Approximate English respelling for English-speaking readers (CAPITALS = stressed syllable; vowels ah / eh / ee / oh / oo; j and g before e/i → kh as in Scottish 'loch'; ñ → ny; ll → y; c before e/i and z → s; r is rolled; monosyllables in capitals). Standard Castilian with seseo and yeísmo. No substitute for the audio.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended.

### L8-en-es-01 · Asking politely (usted) — modèle « ¿Podría {s}, por favor? »

> Polite conditional, usted form. '¿Podría…?' also means 'could I' (next model).

| Phrase | Prononciation | Traduction |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Podría **ayudarme**, por favor? | poh-DREE-ah ah-yoo-DAHR-meh, POHR fah-VOHR | Could you help me, please? |
| ¿Podría **enviarme la factura**, por favor? | poh-DREE-ah ehn-VEEAHR-meh LAH fahk-TOO-rah, POHR fah-VOHR | Could you send me the invoice, please? |
| ¿Podría **hacerme un descuento**, por favor? | poh-DREE-ah ah-SEHR-meh OON dehs-KOOEHN-toh, POHR fah-VOHR | Could you give me a discount, please? |
| ¿Podría **darme más tiempo**, por favor? | poh-DREE-ah DAHR-meh MAHS TEEEHM-poh, POHR fah-VOHR | Could you give me more time, please? |

### L8-en-es-d1 · Haggling at the market

- **A** : Buenos días. ¿Cuánto cuesta esta lámpara? — BOOEH-nohs DEE-ahs. KOOAHN-toh KOOEHS-tah EHS-tah LAHM-pah-rah — Good morning. How much is this lamp?
- **B** : Sesenta euros. — seh-SEHN-tah EHOO-rohs — Sixty euros.
- **A** : Es demasiado caro para mí. ¿Me puede hacer un descuento? — EHS deh-mah-SEEAH-doh KAH-roh PAH-rah MEE. MEH POOEH-deh ah-SEHR OON dehs-KOOEHN-toh — That's too expensive for me. Can you give me a discount?
- **B** : Puedo dejársela en cincuenta. — POOEH-doh deh-KHAHR-seh-lah EHN seen-KOOEHN-tah — I can let you have it for fifty.
- **A** : Si me la deja en cuarenta y cinco, me la llevo. — SEE MEH LAH DEH-khah EHN kooah-REHN-tah EE SEEN-koh, MEH LAH YEH-voh — If you let me have it for forty-five, I'll take it.
- **B** : Vale, pero solo si paga en efectivo. — VAH-leh, PEH-roh SOH-loh SEE PAH-gah EHN eh-fehk-TEE-voh — OK, but only if you pay in cash.
- **A** : De acuerdo. ¿Podría envolverla, por favor? — DEH ah-KOOEHR-doh. poh-DREE-ah ehn-vohl-VEHR-lah, POHR fah-VOHR — All right. Could you wrap it, please?
- **B** : Claro, ahora mismo. — KLAH-roh, ah-OH-rah MEES-moh — Of course, right away.

### L8-en-es-d2 · With the landlord

- **A** : Hola, señor García. Quería preguntarle si puede arreglar la calefacción. — OH-lah, seh-NYOHR gahr-SEE-ah. keh-REE-ah preh-goon-TAHR-leh SEE POOEH-deh ah-rreh-GLAHR LAH kah-leh-fahk-SEEOHN — Hello, Mr García. I wanted to ask you if you can fix the heating.
- **B** : ¿Qué pasa? — KEH PAH-sah — What's wrong?
- **A** : No funciona desde el lunes y hace frío. — NOH foon-SEEOH-nah DEHS-deh EHL LOO-nehs EE AH-seh FREE-oh — It hasn't worked since Monday and it's cold.
- **B** : Lo siento, voy a llamar al técnico hoy mismo. — LOH SEEEHN-toh, VOHEE AH yah-MAHR AHL TEHK-nee-koh OHEE MEES-moh — I'm sorry, I'll call the technician today.
- **A** : Gracias. ¿Sería posible venir antes del viernes? — GRAH-seeahs. seh-REE-ah poh-SEE-bleh veh-NEER AHN-tehs DEHL VEEEHR-nehs — Thank you. Would it be possible to come before Friday?
- **B** : Creo que sí. Si hay algún problema, le llamaré. — KREH-oh KEH SEE. SEE AHEE ahl-GOON proh-BLEH-mah, LEH yah-mah-REH — I think so. If there's any problem, I'll call you.
- **A** : Perfecto. Otra cosa: ¿podría pagar el alquiler el día cinco este mes? — pehr-FEHK-toh. OH-trah KOH-sah, poh-DREE-ah pah-GAHR EHL ahl-kee-LEHR EHL DEE-ah SEEN-koh EHS-teh MEHS — Perfect. One more thing: could I pay the rent on the fifth this month?
- **B** : Vale, pero solo si me lo confirma por escrito. — VAH-leh, PEH-roh SOH-loh SEE MEH LOH kohn-FEER-mah POHR ehs-KREE-toh — OK, but only if you confirm it to me in writing.
- **A** : Sin problema, le envío un correo. — SEEN proh-BLEH-mah, LEH ehn-VEE-oh OON koh-RREH-oh — No problem, I'll send you an email.

### L8-en-es-d3 · Asking for more time at work

- **A** : Marta, ¿tienes un momento? — MAHR-tah, TEEEH-nehs OON moh-MEHN-toh — Marta, do you have a moment?
- **B** : Sí, dime. — SEE, DEE-meh — Yes, go ahead.
- **A** : Necesito dos días más para terminar el informe. — neh-seh-SEE-toh DOHS DEE-ahs MAHS PAH-rah tehr-mee-NAHR EHL een-FOHR-meh — I need another two days to finish the report.
- **B** : ¿Por qué? El plazo es mañana. — POHR KEH. EHL PLAH-soh EHS mah-NYAH-nah — Why? The deadline is tomorrow.
- **A** : Es que el cliente cambió los datos ayer. — EHS KEH EHL KLEEEHN-teh kahm-BEEOH LOHS DAH-tohs ah-YEHR — It's just that the client changed the data yesterday.
- **B** : Vale, pero solo si lo entregas el jueves por la mañana. — VAH-leh, PEH-roh SOH-loh SEE LOH ehn-TREH-gahs EHL KHOOEH-vehs POHR LAH mah-NYAH-nah — OK, but only if you hand it in on Thursday morning.
- **A** : Sí, te lo prometo. Si termino antes, te lo envío. — SEE, TEH LOH proh-MEH-toh. SEE tehr-MEE-noh AHN-tehs, TEH LOH ehn-VEE-oh — Yes, I promise. If I finish earlier, I'll send it to you.
- **B** : De acuerdo. Lo siento, pero no puedo darte más tiempo. — DEH ah-KOOEHR-doh. LOH SEEEHN-toh, PEH-roh NOH POOEH-doh DAHR-teh MAHS TEEEHM-poh — All right. I'm sorry, but I can't give you any more time.
- **A** : Lo entiendo, gracias. — LOH ehn-TEEEHN-doh, GRAH-seeahs — I understand, thanks.

*Acquis attendu* : Ask politely in the conditional (podría, sería posible, le importaría), set a condition (si + present, future), negotiate a price or a deadline, accept on a condition, decline with a reason, apologise and offer to put things right.

*Acquis attendu* : You can make a polite request (您能…吗, 我可以…吗, 能不能, 有没有可能), set a condition (如果… 就…), accept on a condition (要… 才行), decline with an explanation (不好意思，不行，因为…), apologize and offer to fix it (对不起，我马上…), and negotiate a price or a deadline.

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o). The u of de-su / ma-su is almost silent ('dess', 'mass'); r is a light tap between r and l; g is always hard. The reading field gives the hiragana. Polite register (です / ます) throughout.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended; linguistic review required (naturalness of phrasing, kanji readings).

- **B** : 今日はちょっと無理ですが、明日の朝なら行けます。 *(きょうはちょっとむりですが、あしたのあさならいけます。)* — kyo-o wa chot-to mu-ri de-su ga, a-shi-ta no a-sa na-ra i-ke-ma-su — Today is a bit difficult, but I can come tomorrow morning.
- **A** : 分かりました。明日の朝お願いします。 *(わかりました。あしたのあさおねがいします。)* — wa-ka-ri-ma-shi-ta. a-shi-ta no a-sa o-ne-ga-i shi-ma-su — All right. Tomorrow morning, please.
- **B** : はい、九時に行きます。着いたら、電話します。 *(はい、くじにいきます。ついたら、でんわします。)* — ha-i, ku-ji ni i-ki-ma-su. tsu-i-ta-ra, de-n-wa shi-ma-su — Yes, I'll come at nine. When I arrive, I'll call you.
- **A** : ありがとうございます。それから、家賃は来週払ってもいいですか。 *(ありがとうございます。それから、やちんはらいしゅうはらってもいいですか。)* — a-ri-ga-to-o go-za-i-ma-su. so-re-ka-ra, ya-chi-n wa ra-i-shu-u ha-rat-te mo i-i de-su ka — Thank you. Also, may I pay the rent next week?
- **B** : はい、金曜日までなら大丈夫です。 *(はい、きんようびまでならだいじょうぶです。)* — ha-i, ki-n-yo-o-bi ma-de na-ra da-i-jo-o-bu de-su — Yes, but only until Friday.

### L8-en-ja-d3 · At work: asking for more time

- **A** : 田中さん、報告書は今日中にできますか。 *(たなかさん、ほうこくしょはきょうじゅうにできますか。)* — ta-na-ka-sa-n, ho-o-ko-ku-sho wa kyo-o-ju-u ni de-ki-ma-su ka — Mr Tanaka, can the report be done today?
- **B** : すみません、今日はちょっと難しいです。明日の朝まで待っていただけますか。 *(すみません、きょうはちょっとむずかしいです。あしたのあさまでまっていただけますか。)* — su-mi-ma-se-n, kyo-o wa chot-to mu-zu-ka-shi-i de-su. a-shi-ta no a-sa ma-de mat-te i-ta-da-ke-ma-su ka — Sorry, today is a bit difficult. Could you wait until tomorrow morning?
- **A** : うーん、明日の朝なら大丈夫です。でも、十時までにお願いします。 *(うーん、あしたのあさならだいじょうぶです。でも、じゅうじまでにおねがいします。)* — u-u-n, a-shi-ta no a-sa na-ra da-i-jo-o-bu de-su. de-mo, ju-u-ji ma-de ni o-ne-ga-i shi-ma-su — Hmm, tomorrow morning is fine. But by ten o'clock, please.
- **B** : はい、分かりました。終わったら、すぐメールします。 *(はい、わかりました。おわったら、すぐめーるします。)* — ha-i, wa-ka-ri-ma-shi-ta. o-wat-ta-ra, su-gu me-e-ru shi-ma-su — Yes, understood. When I've finished, I'll email you right away.
- **A** : お願いします。 *(おねがいします。)* — o-ne-ga-i shi-ma-su — Thank you.
- **B** : それから、金曜日に休んでもいいですか。 *(それから、きんようびにやすんでもいいですか。)* — so-re-ka-ra, ki-n-yo-o-bi ni ya-su-n-de mo i-i de-su ka — Also, may I take Friday off?
- **A** : はい、でも木曜日までに報告書が終わるなら。 *(はい、でももくようびまでにほうこくしょがおわるなら。)* — ha-i, de-mo mo-ku-yo-o-bi ma-de ni ho-o-ko-ku-sho ga o-wa-ru na-ra — Yes, but only if the report is finished by Thursday.
- **B** : はい、終わります。ありがとうございます。 *(はい、おわります。ありがとうございます。)* — ha-i, o-wa-ri-ma-su. a-ri-ga-to-o go-za-i-ma-su — Yes, it will be. Thank you very much.

*Acquis attendu* : Ask politely with basic keigo (ていただけますか, をお願いできますか), express a condition (たら, なら), ask for a discount, more time or permission (てもいいですか), accept on a condition, decline with a reason (ので、できません) and apologise while putting things right (すみません、すぐ…ます).
