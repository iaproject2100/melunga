# Niveau 2 « Préciser » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Préciser une quantité, une préférence, un lieu ou un moment.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux L1.

## Espagnol (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Approximate English respelling for English-speaking readers (CAPITALS = stressed syllable; vowels ah / eh / ee / oh / oo; j and g before e/i → kh as in Scottish 'loch'; ñ → ny; ll → y; c before e/i and z → s; r is rolled; monosyllables in capitals). Standard Castilian with seseo and yeísmo. No substitute for the audio.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended.

### L2-en-es-d1 · At the restaurant

- **A** : Buenas tardes. ¿Hay mesa libre? — BOOEH-nahs TAHR-dehs. AHEE MEH-sah LEE-breh — Good evening. Is there a free table?
- **B** : Sí, ¿para cuántos? — SEE, PAH-rah KOOAHN-tohs — Yes, for how many?
- **A** : Para dos, por favor. — PAH-rah DOHS, POHR fah-VOHR — For two, please.
- **B** : ¿Aquí o allí, cerca de la ventana? — ah-KEE OH ah-YEE, SEHR-kah DEH LAH vehn-TAH-nah — Here or over there, by the window?
- **A** : Prefiero la ventana. — preh-FEEEH-roh LAH vehn-TAH-nah — I prefer the window.
- **B** : Muy bien. ¿Qué desean? — MOOEE BEEEHN. KEH deh-SEH-ahn — Very good. What would you like?
- **A** : Quisiera dos cafés y un poco de agua. — kee-SEEEH-rah DOHS kah-FEHS EE OON POH-koh DEH AH-gooah — I'd like two coffees and a little water.
- **B** : Ahora mismo. — ah-OH-rah MEES-moh — Right away.

### L2-en-es-d2 · At the station

- **A** : Perdone, ¿a qué hora sale el tren a Madrid? — pehr-DOH-neh, AH KEH OH-rah SAH-leh EHL TREHN AH mah-DREED — Excuse me, what time does the train to Madrid leave?
- **B** : A las ocho y media. — AH LAHS OH-choh EE MEH-deeah — At half past eight.
- **A** : ¿Y cuál es el andén? — EE KOOAHL EHS EHL ahn-DEHN — And which platform is it?
- **B** : El andén cuatro, allí a la derecha. — EHL ahn-DEHN KOOAH-troh, ah-YEE AH LAH deh-REH-chah — Platform four, over there on the right.
- **A** : Quisiera dos billetes, por favor. — kee-SEEEH-rah DOHS bee-YEH-tehs, POHR fah-VOHR — I'd like two tickets, please.
- **B** : ¿Para hoy o para mañana? — PAH-rah OHEE OH PAH-rah mah-NYAH-nah — For today or for tomorrow?
- **A** : Para hoy. ¿Cuánto cuesta? — PAH-rah OHEE. KOOAHN-toh KOOEHS-tah — For today. How much is it?
- **B** : Sesenta euros. — seh-SEHN-tah EHOO-rohs — Sixty euros.

### L2-en-es-d3 · Arranging to meet a friend

- **A** : Hola, Ana. ¿Nos vemos mañana? — OH-lah, AH-nah. NOHS VEH-mohs mah-NYAH-nah — Hi, Ana. Shall we meet tomorrow?
- **B** : Sí, ¿a qué hora? — SEE, AH KEH OH-rah — Yes, what time?
- **A** : ¿A las seis? — AH LAHS SEHEES — At six?
- **B** : Prefiero a las siete, salgo tarde del trabajo. — preh-FEEEH-roh AH LAHS SEEEH-teh, SAHL-goh TAHR-deh DEHL trah-BAH-khoh — I'd rather seven, I leave work late.
- **A** : Vale, a las siete. ¿Dónde? — VAH-leh, AH LAHS SEEEH-teh. DOHN-deh — OK, at seven. Where?
- **B** : En el café de la plaza, cerca del metro. — EHN EHL kah-FEH DEH LAH PLAH-sah, SEHR-kah DEHL MEH-troh — At the café on the square, near the metro.
- **A** : Perfecto, hasta mañana. — pehr-FEHK-toh, AHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah — Perfect, see you tomorrow.
- **B** : ¡Hasta mañana! — AHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah — See you tomorrow!

*Acquis attendu* : Specify a quantity, a place or a time, express a simple preference and ask for a detail (which one, when, where, what time) in a café, a shop, a station or when arranging to meet.

### L2-en-zh-d1 · At the restaurant

- **A** : 你好，有位子吗？ *(nǐ hǎo, yǒu wèizi ma?)* — nee how, yoh way-dzuh mah? — Hello, do you have a table?
- **B** : 有。几位？ *(yǒu. jǐ wèi?)* — yoh. jee way? — Yes. How many people?
- **A** : 两位。我们想要靠窗的座位。 *(liǎng wèi. wǒmen xiǎng yào kào chuāng de zuòwèi.)* — lyahng way. woh-mun shyahng yow kow chwahng duh dzwoh-way. — Two. We'd like a table by the window.
- **B** : 好的，在那里。你们喝什么？ *(hǎo de, zài nàlǐ. nǐmen hē shénme?)* — how duh, dzeye nah-lee. nee-mun huh shun-muh? — Alright, over there. What would you like to drink?

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o). The u of de-su / ma-su is almost silent ('dess', 'mass'); r is a light tap between r and l; g is always hard. The reading field gives the hiragana. Polite register (です / ます) throughout.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended; linguistic review required (naturalness of phrasing, kanji readings).

### L2-en-ja-d3 · Making an appointment
