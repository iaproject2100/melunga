# Niveau 5 « Raconter » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Raconter un événement passé et demander des nouvelles.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux L1, L2, L3, L4.

## Espagnol (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Approximate English respelling for English-speaking readers (CAPITALS = stressed syllable; vowels ah / eh / ee / oh / oo; j and g before e/i → kh as in Scottish 'loch'; ñ → ny; ll → y; c before e/i and z → s; r is rolled; monosyllables in capitals). Standard Castilian with seseo and yeísmo. No substitute for the audio.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended.

### L5-en-es-d1 · Catching up

- **A** : ¡Hola, Pablo! ¿Qué tal? — OH-lah, PAH-bloh. KEH TAHL — Hi, Pablo! How's it going?
- **B** : Muy bien, ¿y tú? ¿Qué hay de nuevo? — MOOEE BEEEHN, EE TOO. KEH AHEE DEH NOOEH-voh — Very well, and you? What's new?
- **A** : El fin de semana fui a Barcelona. — EHL FEEN DEH seh-MAH-nah FOOEE AH bahr-seh-LOH-nah — At the weekend I went to Barcelona.
- **B** : ¿De verdad? ¿Qué hiciste? — DEH vehr-DAHD. KEH ee-SEES-teh — Really? What did you do?
- **A** : Primero visité la Sagrada Familia y luego fui a la playa. — pree-MEH-roh vee-see-TEH LAH sah-GRAH-dah fah-MEE-leeah EE LOOEH-goh FOOEE AH LAH PLAH-yah — First I visited the Sagrada Familia and then I went to the beach.
- **B** : ¡Qué bien! ¿Te gustó la ciudad? — KEH BEEEHN. TEH goos-TOH LAH seeoo-DAHD — Great! Did you like the city?
- **A** : Sí, me encantó. ¿Y tú, qué hiciste? — SEE, MEH ehn-kahn-TOH. EE TOO, KEH ee-SEES-teh — Yes, I loved it. And you, what did you do?
- **B** : Nada especial, descansé en casa. — NAH-dah ehs-peh-SEEAHL, dehs-kahn-SEH EHN KAH-sah — Nothing special, I rested at home.

### L5-en-es-d2 · Monday at the office

- **A** : Buenos días. ¿Qué tal el fin de semana? — BOOEH-nohs DEE-ahs. KEH TAHL EHL FEEN DEH seh-MAH-nah — Good morning. How was your weekend?
- **B** : Regular. El sábado trabajé y el domingo llovió. — reh-goo-LAHR. EHL SAH-bah-doh trah-bah-KHEH EE EHL doh-MEEN-goh yoh-VEEOH — So-so. On Saturday I worked and on Sunday it rained.
- **A** : ¡Qué pena! Yo comí con mis padres y después vimos una película. — KEH PEH-nah. YOH koh-MEE KOHN MEES PAH-drehs EE dehs-POOEHS VEE-mohs OO-nah peh-LEE-koo-lah — What a shame! I had lunch with my parents and afterwards we watched a film.
- **B** : ¿Cuál? — KOOAHL — Which one?
- **A** : Una comedia española. Muy divertida. — OO-nah koh-MEH-deeah ehs-pah-NYOH-lah. MOOEE dee-vehr-TEE-dah — A Spanish comedy. Very funny.
- **B** : ¿Cómo se llama? — KOH-moh SEH YAH-mah — What's it called?
- **A** : No me acuerdo. Te lo digo luego. — NOH MEH ah-KOOEHR-doh. TEH LOH DEE-goh LOOEH-goh — I don't remember. I'll tell you later.
- **B** : Vale, la veo esta semana. — VAH-leh, LAH VEH-oh EHS-tah seh-MAH-nah — OK, I'll watch it this week.

### L5-en-es-d3 · Calling home

- **A** : Hola, mamá. ¿Cómo estás? — OH-lah, mah-MAH. KOH-moh ehs-TAHS — Hi, Mum. How are you?
- **B** : Bien, cansada. Ayer fui al médico. — BEEEHN, kahn-SAH-dah. ah-YEHR FOOEE AHL MEH-dee-koh — Fine, tired. Yesterday I went to the doctor.
- **A** : ¿Y qué te dijo? — EE KEH TEH DEE-khoh — And what did he say?
- **B** : Nada grave. Y tú, ¿qué tal el trabajo? — NAH-dah GRAH-veh. EE TOO, KEH TAHL EHL trah-BAH-khoh — Nothing serious. And you, how's work?
- **A** : Esta mañana tuve una reunión y llegué tarde. — EHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah TOO-veh OO-nah rehoo-NEEOHN EE yeh-KHEH TAHR-deh — This morning I had a meeting and I got there late.
- **B** : ¿Por qué? — POHR KEH — Why?
- **A** : Perdí el autobús. — pehr-DEE EHL ahoo-toh-BOOS — I missed the bus.
- **B** : ¡No me digas! ¿Vienes el domingo? — NOH MEH DEE-gahs. VEEEH-nehs EHL doh-MEEN-goh — No way! Are you coming on Sunday?
- **A** : Sí, voy a comer con vosotros. — SEE, VOHEE AH koh-MEHR KOHN voh-SOH-trohs — Yes, I'm coming to have lunch with you.
- **B** : ¡Qué bien! Hasta el domingo. — KEH BEEEHN. AHS-tah EHL doh-MEEN-goh — Great! See you on Sunday.

*Acquis attendu* : Say what you did (yesterday, at the weekend, on holiday) in the simple past (pretérito indefinido), place events in the past, link actions (primero, luego, después), ask how things are and react.

## Chinois (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : English approximation in the style of the Melunga deck (phe field): dway-boo-chee = 对不起, may gwahn-shee = 没关系, ching = 请. Syllables of one word are joined with hyphens, words are separated by spaces. Tones are not shown: see the pinyin (reading field) and the audio. Standard Mandarin, simplified characters.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended. Linguistic review required (tones, naturalness of phrasing).

### L5-en-zh-01 · Yesterday — modèle « 昨天我{s}了。 »

> 了 at the end of the sentence shows the action is completed, carried through. It is not a past-tense marker: 昨天 is what sets the time. In the negative, use 没 without 了: 昨天我没工作.

| Phrase | Lecture | Prononciation | Traduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 昨天我**看电影**了。 | zuótiān wǒ kàn diànyǐng le. | dzwoh-tyen woh kahn dyen-ing luh. | Yesterday I watched a film. |
| 昨天我**工作**了。 | zuótiān wǒ gōngzuò le. | dzwoh-tyen woh goong-dzwoh luh. | Yesterday I worked. |
| 昨天我**见朋友**了。 | zuótiān wǒ jiàn péngyou le. | dzwoh-tyen woh jyen pung-yoh luh. | Yesterday I saw some friends. |
| 昨天我**休息**了。 | zuótiān wǒ xiūxi le. | dzwoh-tyen woh shyoh-shee luh. | Yesterday I rested. |

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o).
