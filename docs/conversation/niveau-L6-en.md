# Niveau 6 « Projets et propositions » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Parler de projets, proposer et organiser une activité.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux L1, L2, L3, L4, L5.

## Espagnol (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Approximate English respelling for English-speaking readers (CAPITALS = stressed syllable; vowels ah / eh / ee / oh / oo; j and g before e/i → kh as in Scottish 'loch'; ñ → ny; ll → y; c before e/i and z → s; r is rolled; monosyllables in capitals). Standard Castilian with seseo and yeísmo. No substitute for the audio.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended.

### L6-en-es-01 · Saying what you're going to do — modèle « Voy a {s}. »

> 'Voy a + infinitive' = near future; don't confuse it with 'Voy a + place' (L1).

|---|---|---|

### L6-en-es-d1 · Planning an outing

- **A** : ¿Qué vas a hacer este fin de semana? — KEH VAHS AH ah-SEHR EHS-teh FEEN DEH seh-MAH-nah — What are you going to do this weekend?
- **B** : Nada especial. ¿Por qué? — NAH-dah ehs-peh-SEEAHL. POHR KEH — Nothing special. Why?
- **A** : ¿Te apetece ir al cine el sábado? — TEH ah-peh-TEH-seh EER AHL SEE-neh EHL SAH-bah-doh — Do you feel like going to the cinema on Saturday?
- **B** : El sábado no puedo, pero el domingo sí. — EHL SAH-bah-doh NOH POOEH-doh, PEH-roh EHL doh-MEEN-goh SEE — On Saturday I can't, but on Sunday I can.
- **A** : Vale. ¿Quedamos el domingo a las seis? — VAH-leh. keh-DAH-mohs EHL doh-MEEN-goh AH LAHS SEHEES — OK. Shall we meet on Sunday at six?
- **B** : Me parece bien. ¿Dónde? — MEH pah-REH-seh BEEEHN. DOHN-deh — Sounds good to me. Where?
- **A** : En la puerta del cine, en la plaza. — EHN LAH POOEHR-tah DEHL SEE-neh, EHN LAH PLAH-sah — At the cinema entrance, on the square.
- **B** : Perfecto. Te confirmo esta noche. — pehr-FEHK-toh. TEH kohn-FEER-moh EHS-tah NOH-cheh — Perfect. I'll confirm tonight.

### L6-en-es-d2 · Moving a meeting

- **A** : Hola, Luis. La reunión es el lunes a las nueve. — OH-lah, LOOEES. LAH rehoo-NEEOHN EHS EHL LOO-nehs AH LAHS NOOEH-veh — Hi, Luis. The meeting is on Monday at nine.
- **B** : El lunes tengo que ir al dentista. ¿Podríamos cambiar la hora? — EHL LOO-nehs TEHN-goh KEH EER AHL dehn-TEES-tah. poh-DREE-ah-mohs kahm-BEEAHR LAH OH-rah — On Monday I have to go to the dentist. Could we change the time?
- **A** : ¿A las once? — AH LAHS OHN-seh — At eleven?
- **B** : Sí, a las once me va bien. — SEE, AH LAHS OHN-seh MEH VAH BEEEHN — Yes, eleven works for me.
- **A** : ¿En la sala grande o por videollamada? — EHN LAH SAH-lah GRAHN-deh OH POHR vee-deh-oh-yah-MAH-dah — In the big room or by video call?
- **B** : Por videollamada, voy a trabajar desde casa. — POHR vee-deh-oh-yah-MAH-dah, VOHEE AH trah-bah-KHAHR DEHS-deh KAH-sah — By video call, I'm going to work from home.
- **A** : De acuerdo, te envío el enlace. — DEH ah-KOOEHR-doh, TEH ehn-VEE-oh EHL ehn-LAH-seh — All right, I'll send you the link.
- **B** : Gracias, hasta el lunes. — GRAH-seeahs, AHS-tah EHL LOO-nehs — Thanks, see you on Monday.

### L6-en-es-d3 · Buying a train ticket

- **A** : Quiero viajar a Sevilla el viernes. ¿Qué tren hay? — KEEEH-roh veeah-KHAHR AH seh-VEE-yah EHL VEEEHR-nehs. KEH TREHN AHEE — I want to travel to Seville on Friday. What trains are there?
- **B** : Hay uno a las ocho y otro a las diez y media. — AHEE OO-noh AH LAHS OH-choh EE OH-troh AH LAHS DEEEHS EE MEH-deeah — There's one at eight and another at half past ten.
- **A** : Prefiero el de las ocho. ¿De qué andén sale? — preh-FEEEH-roh EHL DEH LAHS OH-choh. DEH KEH ahn-DEHN SAH-leh — I prefer the eight o'clock one. Which platform does it leave from?
- **B** : Del andén tres. ¿Ida y vuelta? — DEHL ahn-DEHN TREHS. EE-dah EE VOOEHL-tah — Platform three. Return ticket?
- **A** : Sí, voy a volver el domingo por la tarde. — SEE, VOHEE AH vohl-VEHR EHL doh-MEEN-goh POHR LAH TAHR-deh — Yes, I'm going to come back on Sunday afternoon.
- **B** : ¿A qué hora le gustaría volver? — AH KEH OH-rah LEH goos-tah-REE-ah vohl-VEHR — What time would you like to come back?
- **A** : Sobre las seis. — SOH-breh LAHS SEHEES — Around six.
- **B** : Hay uno a las seis y cuarto. Son ochenta euros. — AHEE OO-noh AH LAHS SEHEES EE KOOAHR-toh. SOHN oh-CHEHN-tah EHOO-rohs — There's one at a quarter past six. That's eighty euros.
- **A** : Perfecto. Aquí tiene. — pehr-FEHK-toh. ah-KEE TEEEH-neh — Perfect. Here you are.

*Acquis attendu* : Talk about your plans (voy a, pienso, me gustaría, tengo que), suggest an activity, set or move a time and place, accept, decline with an alternative and confirm a plan.

## Chinois (lecteurs anglophones)

Syllables of one word are joined with hyphens, words are separated by spaces. Tones are not shown: see the pinyin (reading field) and the audio. Standard Mandarin, simplified characters.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended. Linguistic review required (tones, naturalness of phrasing).

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o). The u of de-su / ma-su is almost silent ('dess', 'mass'); r is a light tap between r and l; g is always hard. The reading field gives the hiragana. Polite register (です / ます) throughout.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended; linguistic review required (naturalness of phrasing, kanji readings).
