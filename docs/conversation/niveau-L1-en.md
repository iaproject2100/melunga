# Niveau 1 « Premiers contacts » — 7 langues étudiées

Objectif : Saluer, se présenter, exprimer un besoin, commander simplement.

Pour chaque langue : ≥ 10 modèles × 4 variantes (morceau remplaçable en **gras**), 3 dialogues de 6 à 10 répliques. Réutilise les niveaux .

### L1-en-es-d1 · At the café

- **A** : ¡Hola! — OH-lah — Hello!
- **B** : Hola. ¿Qué desea? — OH-lah. KEH deh-SEH-ah — Hello. What would you like?
- **A** : Un café, por favor. — OON kah-FEH, POHR fah-VOHR — A coffee, please.
- **B** : ¿Con leche? — KOHN LEH-cheh — With milk?
- **A** : No, gracias. ¿Cuánto cuesta? — NOH, GRAH-seeahs. KOOAHN-toh KOOEHS-tah — No, thanks. How much is it?
- **B** : Dos euros. — DOHS EHOO-rohs — Two euros.

### L1-en-es-d2 · At the station

- **A** : Perdone, ¿dónde está la estación? — pehr-DOH-neh, DOHN-deh ehs-TAH LAH ehs-tah-SEEOHN — Excuse me, where is the station?
- **B** : Todo recto, a cinco minutos. — TOH-doh REHK-toh, AH SEEN-koh mee-NOO-tohs — Straight ahead, five minutes away.
- **A** : Gracias. Necesito un billete para Valencia. — GRAH-seeahs. neh-seh-SEE-toh OON bee-YEH-teh PAH-rah vah-LEHN-seeah — Thanks. I need a ticket to Valencia.
- **B** : Un billete, diez euros. — OON bee-YEH-teh, DEEEHS EHOO-rohs — One ticket, ten euros.
- **A** : Aquí tiene. Muchas gracias. — ah-KEE TEEEH-neh. MOO-chahs GRAH-seeahs — Here you are. Thank you very much.
- **B** : ¡Buen viaje! — BOOEHN VEEAH-kheh — Have a good trip!

### L1-en-es-d3 · Meeting someone for the first time

- **A** : Hola, me llamo María. — OH-lah, MEH YAH-moh mah-REE-ah — Hi, my name is María.
- **B** : Encantado, yo soy Pablo. — ehn-kahn-TAH-doh, YOH SOHEE PAH-bloh — Nice to meet you, I'm Pablo.
- **A** : Soy francesa, de París. ¿Y usted? — SOHEE frahn-SEH-sah, DEH pah-REES. EE oos-TEHD — I'm French, from Paris. And you?
- **B** : Soy de aquí. ¿Está de vacaciones? — SOHEE DEH ah-KEE. ehs-TAH DEH vah-kah-SEEOH-nehs — I'm from here. Are you on holiday?
- **A** : Sí, me gusta mucho la ciudad. — SEE, MEH GOOS-tah MOO-choh LAH seeoo-DAHD — Yes, I really like the city.
- **B** : ¡Qué bien! ¡Bienvenida! — KEH BEEEHN. beeehn-veh-NEE-dah — Great! Welcome!

*Acquis attendu* : Introduce yourself in three sentences, say what you need, order and ask a price; understand and complete the three dialogues. A learning milestone, not a certification.

- **A** : 你好！ *(nǐ hǎo!)* — nee how! — Hello!
- **B** : 你好。您要什么？ *(nǐ hǎo. nín yào shénme?)* — nee how. neen yow shun-muh? — Hello. What would you like?
- **A** : 请给我一杯咖啡。 *(qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi kāfēi.)* — ching gay woh ee bay kah-fay. — A coffee, please.
- **B** : 加牛奶吗？ *(jiā niúnǎi ma?)* — jyah nyoh-neye mah? — With milk?

### L1-en-zh-d2 · At the station

- **A** : 请问，火车站在哪里？ *(qǐngwèn, huǒchēzhàn zài nǎlǐ?)* — ching-wun, hwoh-chuh-jahn dzeye nah-lee? — Excuse me, where is the train station?

- **A** : 给您。非常感谢。 *(gěi nín. fēicháng gǎnxiè.)* — gay neen. fay-chahng gahn-shyeh. — Here you are. Thank you very much.

## Japonais (lecteurs anglophones)

*Convention phonétique* : Syllable-by-syllable romaji for English readers, in the deck's style (so-o-de-su, ko-wa-i-de-su). Read each syllable as in Spanish or Italian: a as in 'father', i as in 'machine', u as in 'put', e as in 'bet', o as in 'go'. A long vowel is written twice (ko-o-hi-i, to-o-kyo-o); ん is its own beat written n (su-mi-ma-se-n, ko-n-ni-chi-wa); a doubled consonant closes the syllable before it (kip-pu, chot-to, kek-ko-o). The u of de-su / ma-su is almost silent ('dess', 'mass'); r is a light tap between r and l; g is always hard. The reading field gives the hiragana. Polite register (です / ます) throughout.

*Statut* : Written by rule from validated content; native English review recommended; linguistic review required (naturalness of phrasing, kanji readings).
